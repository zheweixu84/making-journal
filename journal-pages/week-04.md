### How might we do AI differently?

I think as a designer to do AI differently, I need to stop treating it like a vending machine that just spits out boring, generic pictures or does my busywork. I need to treat it as a raw, moldable material, just like I did with the live data in my weather and Bitcoin sketches. 

This means my real value isn't just making pretty screens or writing code anymore. It is about setting up the human rules and creative boundaries that guide the AI in the first place. By focusing heavily on empathy to find the right problems to solve. Questioning the biases that AI naturally spits out.

### Cloud AI
When you use tools like ChatGPT, CoPilot, or Gemini, your prompts are sent to remote servers where large models process them and return a response.

This is the opposite of what we just did with Ollama:

 - You need an internet connection
 - Data leaves your machine
 - You’re relying on external infrastructure
 - The models are much larger and more capable

### NotebookLM

1: Build Your Notebook

Create a new notebook at notebooklm.google.com and add sources that represent your experimentation so far on the course.

Mix your own work with other sources:

 - Your Making Journal (paste the URL of your GitHub Pages site)
 - Practitioner works/websites that resonated with you (e.g. Nathalie Miebach, Conditional Design, David Bowen)
 - Data sources or APIs you are curious about

3: Explore in the Chat


Use the chat to ask questions about your sources. Try prompts like:

 - “If my sources were documentation for a design project, what would the final outcome be?”
 - “What do my sources suggest I care about?”
 - “Identify a provocation hidden in my sources”
 - “What would someone who disagrees with the ideas in my sources argue?”

Follow up on anything interesting. Ask it to elaborate, challenge it, or push in a different direction.
